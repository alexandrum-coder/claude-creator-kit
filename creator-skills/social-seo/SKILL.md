# Social SEO: texts that get found in search

People do not find videos only through the feed. Instagram, YouTube and TikTok search the text attached to a video, and Google also indexes public posts from professional accounts. Your job is to put the words people search for in the places the platform reads, in natural language, without keyword stuffing.

## What you ask for at the start (once, then you reuse it)

- What the video or post is about, ideally the transcript or a summary. If you only have the clip, ask for the transcript or run claude-shorts or the YouTube transcript MCP on it.
- Who it is for and where: city or area for local services, for example "antrenor personal Cluj".
- The language of the audience. In Romanian, people often search without diacritics, so the key phrase appears once without them too, for example in a hashtag or in the alt text.
- The account's brand voice, if one exists (brand-voice, brandkit). If none exists, write warm and direct, without hype.

## Step 1. The key phrase (2 minutes, no paid tools)

1. Choose a main phrase of 2–4 words, the way a person types it in search, for example "exerciții abdomen acasă", not "transformă-ți corpul".
2. Add 2–3 secondary phrases: synonyms, the local variant, the problem behind it ("dureri de spate", "postură").
3. Check that people search for them: ask the user to type the start of the phrase into the YouTube and Instagram search bars and send you the autocomplete suggestions. If the user does not have time, mark the phrases as `[neverificat]` and continue.

## Step 2. Instagram (Reels, carousel, post)

- **The first line of the caption** (about the first 125 characters, shown before "more") holds the main phrase and the reason to watch. That sentence decides both search and the click.
- **The body of the caption:** 3–6 short lines. The secondary phrases go in naturally, then a clear call to action: save, send to a friend, ask a question in the comments.
- **On-screen text and subtitles:** the main phrase appears in writing in the first 3 seconds of the reel. Instagram reads the text in the image too. With claude-shorts, the subtitles already carry it.
- **Hashtags:** 3–5 hashtags, specific and relevant, no generic ones like #love or #instagood. Mix one broad hashtag, one niche hashtag and one local hashtag. More is not better. Instagram treats a long list as spam and limits how many hashtags it accepts.
- **Alt text** (Advanced settings → Accessibility): one sentence describing what is in the image, with the main phrase.
- **Location:** add it for local services.
- **The profile** (a one-time setup, worth checking): the Name field holds the main keyword, for example "Ana | Antrenor personal Cluj". The Name field shows up in search; the bio text does not.

## Step 3. YouTube long video

- **Title:** at most about 60 characters, so it is not cut off. The main phrase goes at the start, then the promise. Use no clickbait the video cannot deliver.
- **Description:**
  - **The first 2 lines** (about 150 characters, visible under the video) repeat the main phrase and say what the viewer gets.
  - **Then:** a summary in 3–5 sentences with the secondary phrases, links (site, booking, Instagram), and 3 hashtags at the end. YouTube shows the first 3 above the title.
- **Chapters:** the list starts at `00:00`, has at least 3 chapters, and each chapter lasts at least 10 seconds. Chapter titles are short and contain words people search for. Take the minutes from the transcript, not from memory.
- **Tags:** 5–10, with the main phrase, the variants without diacritics and common misspellings. Tags matter little, so they take only a minute.
- **Thumbnail and on-screen text:** 3–5 large words that do not repeat the title word for word.

## Step 4. YouTube Shorts and TikTok

- **Title / first line:** the main phrase plus the hook, under 100 characters.
- **Description:** 1–2 sentences and 3 hashtags.
- **On-screen text:** the main phrase in the first seconds, as on Instagram.
- If the Short comes from a long video, link the long video (YouTube's "Related video" option).

## What you deliver

For each platform, a ready-to-copy block, in this order:

```
INSTAGRAM
Prima linie: ...
Descriere: ...
Text pe ecran (primele 3 secunde): ...
Hashtaguri: #... #... #...
Alt text: ...

YOUTUBE
Titlu: ...
Descriere (primele 2 rânduri): ...
Descriere (restul): ...
Capitole:
00:00 ...
Tag-uri: ...
```

Under the blocks, one line with the main phrase and the secondary ones, and what is still `[neverificat]`.

## Rules

- Write the text for people first and for search second. Every sentence must read naturally out loud.
- Every keyword must match what the video actually contains. Never promise what the video does not show.
- Health and fitness: no promises of guaranteed results ("minus 10 kg în 2 săptămâni"), no medical advice. Use "poate ajuta", "pentru începători", "consultă un medic dacă ai dureri".
- Write in Romanian with "sunt".
- Do not invent statistics or the number of views.

## Done when

- Each platform the user asked for has its complete block.
- The main phrase appears in the first line, in the on-screen text and in the alt text (Instagram), and in the title and the first 2 lines of the description (YouTube).
- Instagram has at most 5 hashtags. YouTube chapters start at 00:00 and come from the transcript.
